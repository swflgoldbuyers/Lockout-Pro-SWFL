Spare Car Keys: Why Every Driver Should Have One

4 min read · Updated 2026-08-07

  1. Home
  2. Resources
  3. Spare Car Keys: Why Every Driver Should Have One

A spare car key is not a luxury. It's the simplest way to avoid emergency lockouts, lost-key chaos, and expensive all-keys-lost programming.

Need help now? Call (239) 380-5240 for mobile automotive locksmith service across Southwest Florida.

One Key = One Point Of Failure

Drop it in a lake, leave it at the beach, or lock it inside — and your day stops.

Modern Keys Need Programming

A spare must actually start the car. That often means cutting plus programming, not a bargain blade copy.

Best Time To Get A Spare

While you still have a working key. All-keys-lost jobs are harder and usually more stressful.

Where To Keep It

At home or with a trusted person — not inside the vehicle.

SWFL Reality Check

Heat, beaches, tourism, and busy parking lots make lockouts common. A spare is local common sense.
