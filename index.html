FAQ

Questions Drivers Ask Mid-Emergency

Do you only work on vehicles?

Yes. Lockout Pro SWFL is automotive-only. For residential or commercial locksmith needs, visit A Good Locksmith.

How fast can you arrive for a car lockout?

Arrival time depends on your location and current calls. When you call (239) 380-5240, we provide a realistic estimate for your Southwest Florida area.

Can you replace lost car keys and program fobs?

In many cases yes. We handle lost key replacement, key fob programming, smart keys, and push-to-start keys for a wide range of vehicles.

What areas do you serve?

Fort Myers, Cape Coral, Naples, Bonita Springs, Estero, North Naples, Fort Myers Beach, Lehigh Acres, San Carlos Park, North Fort Myers, and surrounding SWFL communities.

Will unlocking my car damage the door?

We use professional automotive entry methods intended to minimize risk. Improvised DIY tools are far more likely to damage seals, paint, or linkages.
