Frequently Asked Questions

Should I try to dig the key out myself?

No. Improvised picks can push fragments deeper or damage the cylinder.

Can you extract a key from the ignition?

Yes — ignition and door lock extractions are core automotive locksmith work.

Will I need a new ignition?

Not always. Many extractions leave the cylinder usable. We'll assess after removal.

Can you make a new key from the broken pieces?

Often yes, especially if enough of the original key remains or vehicle codes are available.
